## Suggested order
1. **Core AI:** the ⭐ papers, especially GNNs, equivariance and diffusion. Everything else builds on these.
2. **Materials and chemistry:** in parallel, depending on interest.
3. **Scientific ML:** when you need PDE or continuum methods.
4. **The frontier stages of every list:** the 2025–26 papers.
