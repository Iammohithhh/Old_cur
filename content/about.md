**Curiosophy** is a learning notebook, not a publication. Its main audience is me. If it helps someone else too, that's a bonus.

## How pages get made

1. I explore a question wherever it's comfortable: Google Docs, Notion, paper notes, or a conversation with an AI assistant.
2. The rough material goes into a private inbox.
3. An AI assistant turns it into a clean page, with equations, references, code and a list of claims I should check.
4. I review the page. Only after I've checked it does its status change from `ai-draft` to `reviewed`.

So AI does a lot of the drafting and formatting. The questions, the judgement about what's right, and the decision about what stays are mine.

## Labels

- **status:** `ai-draft` (not yet checked by me) or `reviewed` (checked and understood).
- **code:** `ran` (executed, real output), `untested` (a suggestion only) or `none`.
- **open-questions:** what I still don't understand, so I have something to come back to.

## Corrections

Pages change as my understanding improves. When I find I misunderstood something, I fix the page and note it in the [[log]] rather than quietly deleting it.
